The Artist

I’m Kamal Raj, the artist behind Lucid Lines. I design and execute hand-drawn, highly detailed murals that bring rhythm and story to premium spaces—hotels, clubs, cafes, gyms and private residences. My signature is a fusion of intricate doodle lattices and visionary, psychedelic forms, often extended with UV-reactive layers for night-time transformations. Each wall is site-specific—no templates, no stock patterns.

Originals, Not Templates

Every composition is drawn from scratch for your architecture and brand narrative. You get a one-of-one piece with usage rights, not a repeatable print.

Built for Real Spaces

Surface prep, archival paints and clear-coat options ensure durability. UV layers can create day–night ambience shifts for hospitality.

Collaborative & Reliable

Transparent proposals, moodboards and phased approvals. I coordinate with architects and facility teams to deliver clean, on-schedule installs.
