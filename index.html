Where Psychedelic Art Meets Urban Walls

The Artist

I'm Kamal Raj, a versatile, self-taught artist specializing in detailed doodles, psychedelic murals, and vibrant UV paintings. My passion is to transform spaces and bring visions to life. Through my art group, the Greenape Project, and collaborations with numerous artists, I've honed a unique style that blends intricate detail with bold, mind-bending visuals. I have extensive experience with commissioned art, creating commercial graffiti and custom pieces for coffee shops, hotels, restaurants, gyms, pubs, and residential spaces.
